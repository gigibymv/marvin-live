# Revenue resilience

Illustrative MARVIN report. All figures below are sample inputs, not research about a real company. This file demonstrates the report structure; it was authored for the landing-page example.

## Assignment

Assess revenue growth, customer concentration and retention for a software investment review.

## Supplied sample inputs

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| 2024 revenue | €8.0m |
| 2025 revenue | €10.0m |
| Largest customer share | 45% of revenue |
| Customer retention | Not supplied |

## What the figures show

Revenue increased by 25%: €10.0m / €8.0m - 1 = 0.25.

The largest customer represents 45% of revenue. The investment therefore depends heavily on one customer relationship.

## What remains unverified

Customer retention data was not supplied. Growth alone does not establish that customers stay. Obtain retention figures before concluding that growth is durable.
